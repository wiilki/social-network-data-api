Available routes

GET/api/usersList or search profiles
POST/api/usersCreate a profile
GET / PUT / DELETE/api/users/:idRead, update email, or delete
POST / DELETE/api/users/:userId/friends/:friendIdManage friendships
GET / POST/api/thoughtsList or publish thoughts
GET / PUT / DELETE/api/thoughts/:idRead, edit, or remove a thought
POST/api/thoughts/:thoughtId/reactionsReact to a thought
DELETE/api/thoughts/:thoughtId/reactions/:reactionIdRemove a reaction
